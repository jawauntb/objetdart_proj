# post-validation horizon

**Status: not implemented.** This is the executable registry of full-vision
families outside Release 1. A horizon item may leave this document only when a
later release embodies it and records validated promotion evidence under
`docs/native/validation-promotions/<family-id>.md`.

Release 1 is exactly `wave`, `cell`, and `solar`. It proves a foundation; it
does not make the remaining universe a backlog, a promise of parity, or a
finished destination in the fold.

## promotion evidence

A promotion record must have YAML frontmatter containing all of:

```yaml
family: horizon-family-id
validated: true
embodiedRelease: <v2-or-later>
simulationContract: path-or-contract-id
scientificReview: path-or-evidence-id
comprehensionEvidence: path-or-evidence-id
accessibilityEvidence: path-or-evidence-id
persistenceEvidence: path-or-evidence-id
performanceEvidence: path-or-evidence-id
universeRelationship: one-sentence-causal-link
```

The item is still a horizon until that record exists and the native scope test
accepts it. A design mock, a route, or a source file is not promotion evidence.

## required families

### cosmic-deep-time

<!-- native-horizon: cosmic-deep-time -->

Quantum fluctuations as bounded instruments; Big Bang conditions, expansion,
nucleosynthesis, first stars, stellar lifecycles, supernovae, heavy elements,
galaxies, cosmic web, black holes, and deep-space navigation.

### particle-and-nuclear-matter

<!-- native-horizon: particle-and-nuclear-matter -->

Photons and fields, quarks and flavor, gluons, hadrons, nuclei, decay, fusion,
fission, and element formation, with explicit limits between visualization and
tractable quantum model.

### atoms-and-chemistry

<!-- native-horizon: atoms-and-chemistry -->

Electron structure, periodic behavior, bonding, molecular geometry,
thermodynamics, kinetics, catalysis, reaction networks, organic precursors,
and chemical environments.

### abiogenesis-and-molecular-biology

<!-- native-horizon: abiogenesis-and-molecular-biology -->

Autocatalysis, compartments, metabolism, replication, RNA, DNA, genes,
recombination, chromosomes, proteins, folding, organelles, viruses, bacteria,
archaea, eukaryogenesis, and endosymbiosis.

### multicellular-life

<!-- native-horizon: multicellular-life -->

Tissues, morphogenesis, development, plants, fungi, animals, nervous systems,
behavior, reproduction, selection, drift, extinction, and ecological
succession.

### planetary-worlds

<!-- native-horizon: planetary-worlds -->

Planet formation beyond the solar proof; interiors, tectonics, mountains,
erosion, soil, atmosphere, climate, weather, oceans, coasts, organisms,
generated biomes, and explorable maps.

### mathematical-and-law-instruments

<!-- native-horizon: mathematical-and-law-instruments -->

Light and color-sound mappings, circularity and Fourier analysis, symmetry,
quotient, groups, eigen behavior, topology, probability, relativity,
information, constraint, and other phenomenon-derived felt proofs.

### memory-and-shared-worlds

<!-- native-horizon: memory-and-shared-worlds -->

Deeper natural history, compare and replay, shareable deterministic states,
collaborative branches, public artifacts, and social presence. Commutative
merging waits for measured conflict evidence.

### platform-extensions

<!-- native-horizon: platform-extensions -->

Apple Pencil hover and pressure, spatial audio, widgets, Live Activities,
visionOS, macOS, Android feasibility, controllers, and installation-scale
performance tiers.

### web-room-reassessment

<!-- native-horizon: web-room-reassessment -->

Evaluate each existing web route as a law, material, lens, memory surface, or
superseded sketch. Deepen or merge it into a horizon family; never port it
solely because it exists.
