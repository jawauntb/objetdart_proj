the answer

Grounding Context

The strongest use of the paper is not to install its Fourier neural operator in the apps. Use its central law—an electronic state should be reached through visible, revisable self-consistency—to deepen one molecule room. Let a hand perturb a bounded cause, let the field gather and correct itself, and let failure remain a legitimate ending.

That move fits the work already present. The web atom and molecule rooms are deterministic scientific stylizations, while native AtomKernel explicitly disclaims being a quantum solver and MoleculeKernel uses a curated register. Native already has scalar surfaces, fixed-step simulation, checkpoint promotion, and quarantine; neither client currently ships an ML runtime. The paper therefore offers a better behavior and evidence shape before it offers a deployable model.

The scientific boundary matters. The supplied August 24 manuscript reports a learned potential→density map inside a retained self-consistent field cycle, trained across 8,504 molecular and solid examples. It also reports that its unfine-tuned model diverged on every tested magnesium-defect case. As of August 25, the paper is author-hosted and no indexed publication, public code, weights, browser deployment, or phone deployment was found. Convergence is useful evidence, but it is not an accuracy certificate.

primary recommendation

Prototype “the self-consistent molecule” on one canonical molecule, fed by an offline reference-field cassette and recorded as a renderer-free trace. WebGL and Metal may interpret the same scientific score differently; the score, residual milestones, validity envelope, and refusal state remain shared.

shared authority

seed, semantic interventions, reference-field cassette, logical iterations, residual milestones, validity disposition, and a versioned field-quantization rule

web expression

WebGL field reconstruction, Web Audio beating, gesture grammar, keyboard and reduced-motion paths, 390px evidence

iOS expression

Metal field reconstruction, haptic tension and release, vessel response, fixed-step authority, thermal and memory evidence

1.

The self-consistent molecule

playable self-consistency confidence 80% complexity medium
Description
In one molecule room, the hand perturbs nuclei or a bounded effective-potential well—it never paints the density. A deterministic authored loop lets the electronic field gather, overshoot, mix, and settle. Residual drives moiré, audio beating, and haptic tension. A longer hold reveals later corrections and may still end unresolved.
Basis
direct: docs/gesture-grammar.md and AGENTS.md make duration continuous and require meaningful acts to land through shared visual, audio, and haptic buses. external: the Khan et al. manuscript keeps potential construction, density updates, mixing, a convergence residual, and an explicit unconverged outcome. reasoned: residual can become sensory tension without being presented as physical electron time.
Rationale
This converts the paper’s central architectural insight into an instrument. Most electronic visualizers show a finished field; this room makes correction itself playable while preserving the distinction between hand, cause, and electronic response.
Downsides
Solver iteration is not physical electron time. The guide and sensory timing must avoid that misconception, and a reference trajectory is needed before the motion can claim electronic meaning.
Proof gate
In a silent 30-second test, a visitor should distinguish perturbation, settling, a residual spike, recovery, and refusal. The same seed must reproduce exact semantic action and logical-tick ordering; numeric fields must agree within declared tolerances.
2.

The electronic field press

runtime delivery envelope confidence 80% complexity high
Description
An offline compiler turns conventional SCF trajectories—or later, a reproducible and licensed learned solver—into content-addressed field cassettes. Each cassette carries compact basis fields, coefficient tracks, residuals, invariants, a validity envelope, provenance, fixtures, and disclosures. WebGL and Metal reconstruct it locally with no runtime model.
Basis
direct: scripts/native/generate-reference-fixtures.mjs and docs/plans/2026-08-19-1352-feat-native-cosmogony-garden-plan.md establish versioned offline scientific fixtures while keeping runtime state deterministic. external: ONNX Runtime Web and Core ML make inference possible in principle, but the Khan et al. manuscript provides no released model or mobile/browser envelope.
Rationale
This captures useful electronic behavior now while protecting offline play, startup, thermal headroom, frame budgets, and scientific review. The paper can later become one press input without becoming the app’s authority by decree.
Downsides
Compression fidelity, source licensing, reference-compute cost, and authoring ergonomics are all unproven. “Cassette” is a hypothesis to measure, not a promised format.
Proof gate
One cassette must load instantly offline, reconstruct a trajectory within a declared reference tolerance, and pass browser plus device frame, memory, and thermal evidence.
3.

Calm is not trust

trust and failure confidence 90% complexity medium
Description
Replace binary “stable” with scientific dispositions: converged-in-domain, residual-rising, maximum-iterations, domain-unknown, and reference-unverified. Residual controls one sensory layer; domain support or model disagreement controls another. Only explicitly permitted states promote, and the last trustworthy checkpoint remains when a run is quarantined.
Basis
direct: packages/objet-universe-kit/Sources/ObjetUniverseCore/SimulationProtocol.swift exposes stable: Bool, while UniverseHost.swift quarantines non-stable output. Current atom and molecule checks largely establish finite-number validity. external: the Khan et al. manuscript treats residual as a convergence signal and reports magnesium transfer failure; Mouli et al. show that neural-operator uncertainty under distribution shift is a separate problem.
Rationale
Both apps gain an honest trust grammar without badges or diagnostic panels inside the room. The visitor feels whether the field is still working, unsupported despite calm, or refused; the guide names the distinction when asked.
Downsides
A defensible support or disagreement measure must be calibrated. Purely aesthetic failure signals can remain ambiguous, so guide language and play testing matter.
Proof gate
Visitors should distinguish “still settling,” “settled but unsupported,” and “quarantined” without labels, while tests prove promotion rules and last-good rollback.
4.

One molecule, no excuses

electronic-field fidelity confidence 80% complexity medium
Description
Limit the first proof to H₂ or one canonical molecule in a declared bond-length and pose envelope. Back every allowed state with reference fields, electron-count conservation, pose equivariance, and explicit refusal outside the envelope. Spend fidelity on depth rather than expanding the catalog.
Basis
direct: packages/objet-universe-kit/Sources/ObjetUniverseCore/Atoms/AtomKernel.swift explicitly disclaims quantum solving. external: the Khan et al. manuscript’s magnesium results make broad out-of-distribution transfer an explicit evidence boundary. reasoned: a narrow physical domain makes charge conservation, pose behavior, interpolation, and refusal falsifiable.
Rationale
This creates one defensible fidelity anchor and the smallest experiment that can decide whether electronic fields genuinely improve the artwork.
Downsides
The first release deepens only one molecule. Choosing the reference method, basis, grid, validity interval, and wording requires scientific review.
Proof gate
Every approved bond and pose state must match references, conserve charge, replay on both platforms, and refuse unsupported states.
5.

The portable electronic trace

cross-platform state and replay confidence 80% complexity high
Description
Persist a renderer-free score: seed, model or fixture version, semantic interventions, logical ticks, iteration packets, residual milestones, dispositions, checkpoints, and a versioned quantization rule for field digests. Swift and TypeScript render differently but replay the same semantic event order; continuous numeric fields compare within declared tolerances.
Basis
direct: docs/native/simulation-contract.md and packages/objet-universe-kit/Sources/ObjetUniverseCore/ScenarioTrace.swift establish renderer-free, versioned actions, traces, checkpoints, recovery, and cross-language comparison. They are not yet the live molecule authority. external: the Khan et al. manuscript makes intermediate iteration milestones worth preserving rather than storing only an endpoint.
Rationale
A molecular act can remain the same artwork across browser and phone instead of merely looking similar. It also produces inspectable regression evidence without forcing the renderers into lockstep.
Downsides
The quantization rule, numeric tolerances, event-version migrations, persistence wiring, and platform floating-point differences make this a substantial contract project.
Proof gate
A web-exported score must reproduce exact semantic event ordering plus the same residual and disposition milestones on iOS across relaunch and frame tiers. Continuous fields must stay within tolerance; digests compare only after both clients apply the same versioned quantization.
6.

Make model migration a room lens

cross-platform state and replay confidence 70% complexity medium
Description
Every model or fixture upgrade emits paired, same-seed traces from current and candidate authorities. A twist moves through both fields and their difference; interference, beating, and haptics expose drift. The same paired artifact supports automated regression checks, scientific review, and—if it stands as material—a visitor-facing lens.
Basis
direct: docs/native/simulation-contract.md requires a model-version or evidence change rather than a silent fixture refresh, and docs/gesture-grammar.md assigns representation changes to the lens layer. reasoned: endpoint calm can hide scientific drift, while paired deterministic traces isolate the changed authority without changing the underlying visitor state.
Rationale
Normally invisible model migration becomes audible, visible, and reviewable. One artifact may pay for both quality control and art.
Downsides
This is downstream of two credible authorities and may feel like a developer diff tool if the comparison lacks intrinsic material interest.
Proof gate
An intentionally altered candidate must be caught by the paired trace and felt through the lens without running two solvers in the frame loop.
7.

The reciprocal-space spectrograph

electronic-field fidelity confidence 70% complexity medium
Description
A two-finger lens turns one approved density field between real-space fog and physical-radius reciprocal bands. Pose rotates the real field coherently while the spectral relationships remain stable across the approved domain. The lens interprets the same authority; it never invents a second state.
Basis
direct: src/components/AtomsField.tsx already contains a representation-lens precedent. external: the Khan et al. operator uses physical reciprocal-radius filters plus SE(3)-equivariant structure. reasoned: a real/reciprocal contrast can expose the same field’s spatial-frequency structure and pose relationships without adding explanatory labels.
Rationale
This could make pose equivariance and scale structure physical without embedding the paper’s model, claiming that a Fourier view itself proves nonlocal response, or adding in-room copy.
Downsides
It can become a classroom spectrogram. The room needs a reference-backed field and an interaction whose sensory reward survives without labels.
Proof gate
Rotation and domain tests must preserve declared spectral relationships, and play testing must read as “the same field under another lens,” not “a new chart.”
